## Two ways to use Mizan

**Simple** keeps to the basics: your cash accounts, what comes in and goes out, a month report, exchange rates and debts. **Advanced** adds the plan and its buckets, bills, income sources, goals, gold and silver, other assets, the net worth history and the household.

You choose when you create your account, and you can change it any time in **Settings → Simple or advanced**. The choice follows you to every device you sign in on.

## What switching does

Switching only changes what is shown. Nothing is deleted. If you recorded gold, a goal or a plan in advanced mode and then switch to simple, they are still there when you switch back.

Your net worth on Home always counts everything you recorded, in both modes, so the number does not change when you switch. In simple mode a short note under it says when it includes gold or other assets from advanced mode.

## Recording money in simple mode

**Add transaction** asks for the amount, whether it came in or went out, the account, and a category if you want one. There are no buckets. The **Money in and out** card on Home shows what came in, what went out and what is left.

To move money between two of your accounts use **Cash → Transfer**. To record a loan or a repayment use **Debts**.

## A screen says it is part of advanced mode

An old link or bookmark can open a screen that simple mode leaves out. Mizan says so and offers **Switch to advanced**.

## Sharing a household

Inviting family members is part of advanced mode. Each person chooses their own mode, so one of you can use simple and the other advanced on the same records.
