Mizan has no bank connection, so cash is a set of balances you maintain: a wallet, a bank account, cash at home, a mobile wallet. Enter the balance whenever you check it. Each change is logged with the previous and new amounts and an optional reason, so you can see when a balance moved.

## Income and spending move the balance

When you mark income received you can say which accounts it went into, and an expense or a paid bill can say which account it was paid from. The account's balance moves with them: it is the balance you last set, plus linked income and minus linked spending since. The account shows what moved it since you last set it. When you count your money and enter the real balance, that becomes the new starting point and everything before it is taken in, so nothing is counted twice.

## Moving money between accounts

**Transfer** on the Cash screen moves money from one account to another, such as a withdrawal from the bank into the wallet. Between currencies, enter what you sent and what you actually received, so an exchange at a money changer is recorded at the rate you got. A transfer is not income or spending: the plan and the report are not touched. It shows in the ledger as one entry, such as "Dollars → Wallet", which can be edited or deleted.

Accounts in other currencies are converted at the rate valuation uses for the net worth figure. Mark an account **Private to me** to keep it out of other members' lists once households are shared.
