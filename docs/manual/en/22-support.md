## Sending a report

Open **More → Report a problem** and press **New report**. Choose whether it is a problem, a suggestion or a question, give it a short subject and describe what you did, what you expected and what you saw instead.

You can attach up to four screenshots or photos. Mizan shrinks them on your device before sending, and removes the location and camera details from them. Photos are deleted six months after a report is resolved; the thread then says a photo was there.

## Who can read it

Reports are not end-to-end encrypted like your records: the people who run Mizan read what you write and the images you attach, because that is how they help. Do not include amounts, account numbers or anything else you want to keep private. A report never includes your records unless you type them in or show them in a screenshot.

## Replies

Replies appear under the report and you get a notification on every device where notifications are on. A report shows **Open**, **In progress** or **Resolved**. Replying to a resolved report opens it again.

You can send up to five new reports and thirty replies a day.
