## Display

Switch between English and Arabic at any time. In Arabic you can choose Western or Eastern Arabic digits. Light and dark themes follow the system by default.

**Show amounts in** shows totals in another currency at today's rate, on this device only: for example your dinar net worth in dollars. Converted amounts are marked ≈ and an "In USD" badge appears by the sync status; tap it to go back. Nothing saved changes, and amounts are still entered in their own currency.

## Dates

**Show Hijri dates alongside** adds the Umm al-Qura date next to the Gregorian one in the ledger, the month picker and the asset and zakat screens.

Digits, Hijri dates, the net worth in US dollars and the asset revaluation reminder follow you: change one on the phone and the web app picks it up at its next sync. Language, theme and **Show amounts in** stay on each device.

## Security

Set how long the app may stay in the background before it locks, switch fingerprint or face unlock on or off for this device, lock it now, or remove the PIN.

## Country and currency

Change your country here; it decides whether you see the Baghdad market screens. The household owner can also change the main currency. Every amount for the whole household (totals, plans, past snapshots and the rate saved with each record) is converted at that day's rate. Records keep the currency they were entered in. Changing back later uses the later day's rate, so numbers will not return exactly. Other members' devices convert their private records the next time they sync.

## Household

Rename the household and set the day the month starts on.

## Your data

**Export JSON** downloads everything, including the recycle bin, as one file. **Import JSON** checks a file before writing anything and shows what it contains; imported rows sync like your own edits.

## Deleting your account

**Delete my account** removes the account and the household 30 days after you confirm. Until then you can still sign in to export or to cancel. Export first if you want a copy.
