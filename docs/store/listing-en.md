## Description
Mizan gives you one number for what you're worth, and a plan that keeps it growing.

- Plan every dinar: give each month's income a job, move money between buckets, and see what is left.
- Track spending in seconds: quick add, split a purchase, attach a receipt photo.
- Know your net worth: cash, gold and silver at today's prices, other assets with depreciation, and debts, with a month close that explains what moved the number.
- Gold, silver and zakat: lots, sales, dealer products and a zakat estimate.
- Bills, subscriptions, income and goals with reminders.
- Share a household: invite members with roles, or give an advisor read-only access.
- Arabic and English, light and dark, Hijri dates and Arabic digits.

Your records are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they are synced. Mizan's servers
store only ciphertext and cannot read your amounts, payees or notes. Lock the app with a PIN,
Face ID or your fingerprint.
